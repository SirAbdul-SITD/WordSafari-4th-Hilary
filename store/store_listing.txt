APP TITLE (max 50 chars):
Linkoro: Number Link Flow

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80 chars):
Connect matching numbers with paths that fill the whole grid! 150 levels.

FULL DESCRIPTION:
🔗 CONNECT. FILL. FLOW. 🔗

Link each pair of matching numbers with a colored path. The paths can't cross — and together they must fill every single cell. Find the one arrangement where everything connects and nothing is left empty.

🎯 HOW TO PLAY
• Drag from a number to its matching twin
• Paths can't cross or overlap
• Cover every cell to complete the puzzle
• Drag over an existing path to redraw it

✨ FEATURES
• 150 levels: 5×5, 6×6 and 7×7 grids
• Every puzzle is carved from real filling paths — always solvable
• Smooth color-coded flow drawing
• Live linked/filled progress counter
• Crisp neon-on-slate visuals and a smooth soundtrack
• Haptic feedback
• 100% offline, no ads, no purchases, zero data collection

Also known as Numberlink, Flow, or Arukone — a beloved connect-the-pairs puzzle. The twist: every cell must be used, so there's exactly one elegant way to weave them all.
